PRACTICE DEFINITION

What We Mean by Bench Sales

US IT Bench Sales is the process of marketing and positioning available technology consultants for relevant contract and staffing opportunities in the US market.

At TJR Tech, the process focuses on presenting a consultant's technical background clearly, aligning the profile with relevant requirements, communicating with staffing contacts, coordinating submissions, and supporting the consultant through interview and placement stages.

COMPLIANCE CLARITY

Professional Profile Information Matters.

Work authorization and availability are important components of US IT staffing. Consultant profiles should clearly communicate applicable work authorization information so opportunities can be evaluated appropriately.

OPT / STEM OPT H-1B H-4 EAD GC EAD US Citizen Permanent Resident

Work authorization requirements vary by opportunity. Consultants should verify eligibility and employment requirements for each specific position. TJR Tech does not provide immigration or legal advice.
