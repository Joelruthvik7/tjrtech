OUR PROCESS

From Requirement to Placement.

A disciplined six-stage methodology ensuring operational compliance and candidate quality.

01

REQUIREMENT DISCOVERY

We understand the role, technical requirements, engagement model, location expectations, timeline, and other relevant requirements.

02

TALENT SOURCING

We identify technology professionals through recruitment channels, professional networks, candidate databases, and referrals.

03

SCREENING & QUALIFICATION

04

PROFILE POSITIONING

05

SUBMISSION & INTERVIEW

06

Once selected, we support the applicable onboarding and coordination process through the beginning of the engagement.
