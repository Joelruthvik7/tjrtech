OUR STORY

From Practical Experience to a New Digital Organization.

The purpose is to build an independent organization with its own professional identity, structured operations, long-term relationships, and scalable technology capabilities.

TJR Tech is being established to bring together practical experience in US IT staffing, technical recruitment, consultant coordination, business operations, and digital development under one professional corporate identity.

The team has previously worked across US IT staffing workflows, candidate sourcing, consultant communication, placement coordination, and digital development through private business arrangements.

These experiences shaped the foundation for TJR Tech. Built on direct operational execution, the organization prioritizes predictability, relationship integrity, and engineering rigor over unnecessary complexity.
